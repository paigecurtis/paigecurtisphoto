About Me

I'm a people person, and art is my greatest passion. I'm a photographer and artist based in Oahu, Hawaii, originally from California. My love for people is immense and rooted in my faith. I love photographing people because I love to capture them in their most authentic and joyous states—whether that's a couple in love, a family laughing together, or a maternity session full of hope.

I've had a passion for art for as long as I can remember. I grew up with a sketchbook in my hand and a disposable camera in my pocket, and that love for capturing moments has stayed with me ever since. I've always been drawn to the outdoors and find so much inspiration in nature—the light, the waves, and the way people connect in these spaces.

I live in Oahu, Hawaii, but I'm willing to travel anywhere for sessions. When I'm not behind the camera, you'll find me with my family—my husband (a helicopter pilot) and our golden retriever, Rotor. I love surfing, hiking, and just hanging out with my boys.

If you have any questions or want to book a portrait, couple, or family session in Hawaii or beyond, please reach out!
